A Bit More Underground Unisex Tee

Celebrate your love for raw, unfiltered music with the A Bit More Underground Unisex Tee. Designed for those who value authenticity over popularity, this shirt champions the values of those who seek out beats and veues outside the mainstream.

Made with soft, premium cotton, this tee offers comfort and durability, perfect for warehouse parties, casual streetwear, or wherever the path less traveled takes you. Let your style reflect your passion for music that resonates beyond the mainstream.

• 100% cotton, Airlume combed and ring-spun.

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for consistent sizing.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Tear-away label for added comfort.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for a polished finish.
