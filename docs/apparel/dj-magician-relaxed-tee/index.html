DJ Magician Tee

Step into a world of mysticism and music with The DJ Magician Tee! This captivating design features a tarot-inspired "The Magician" card reimagined as a mystical DJ, blending the power of rhythm and magic. The back of the shirt showcases the intricate back of a tarot card along with the Temple of Unicorns logo, adding a touch of celestial charm.

Crafted from smooth, premium polyester jersey, this tee is designed for comfort, style, and durability. Whether you’re spinning beats, dancing under the stars, or just channeling your inner magician, this tee keeps your vibe enchanting.

• Fabric composition: 93% polyester, 7% spandex

• Fabric weight: 7.08 oz./yd.² (240 g/m²)

• Premium knit mid-weight jersey

• Smooth, comfortable 2-way stretch fabric that stretches and recovers on the cross and lengthwise grains

• Regular fit

• OEKO-TEX 100 standard certified fabric
