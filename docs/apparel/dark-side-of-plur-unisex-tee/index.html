Dark Side of PLUR Unisex Tee

At Temple of Unicorns, we celebrate the full spectrum of rave culture, from its brightest beats to its deepest, darkest basslines. The Dark Side of PLUR Unisex Tee is a tribute to those who embrace the shadowy edges of electronic music while still living the values of Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect.

This tee is for the ravers who find their groove in moody atmospheres, haunting melodies, and pounding bass — not because they’re turning away from the light, but because they know the dark is just as essential to the balance. Crafted with comfort and durability in mind, this tee lets you represent your unique taste and prove that true PLUR is about inclusivity and respect for all styles. Show the world that even the darkest beats can radiate unity and respect. Because at Temple of Unicorns, we know there's room at the rave for everyone.

• 100% cotton, Airlume combed and ring-spun.

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for consistent sizing.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Tear-away label for added comfort.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for a polished finish.
