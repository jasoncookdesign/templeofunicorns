Love Hard Crest Micro-Rib Tank

Wear your heart on your sleeve (or chest) with the Love Hard Crest Micro-Rib Tank! Featuring a striking heraldic emblem centered on a realistic human heart adorned by PLUR symbols, this design is a bold reminder to embrace life’s challenges with passion and resilience. The motto "Life’s Hard Love Hard" adds a powerful message of strength and love to this unique design.

Crafted from soft, durable fabric, the Love Hard Crest Micro-Rib Tank is perfect for festivals, casual outings, or wherever you want to inspire and express yourself. Let this tee remind you to live fiercely and love deeply, no matter what life throws your way.

• 100% Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton.

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Relaxed fit with wide, low-cut armholes.

• Side-seamed construction for a better fit.

• Tear-away label for added comfort.
