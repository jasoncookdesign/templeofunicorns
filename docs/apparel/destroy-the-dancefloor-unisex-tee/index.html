Celebrate the artistry of the battle DJ with the Destroy the Dancefloor Unisex Tee! Featuring bold graphics and commanding text, this tee honors the iconic tool of turntablists who redefine musical boundaries. From precise cuts to jaw-dropping routines, this shirt is a tribute to those who wield their mixers with skills mere mortals can only witness with amazement, turning every performance into a battle to destroy the dancefloor. Show your respect for turntablism and join the battle-ready ranks of DJs who slay with every scratch.

Crafted from soft, lightweight fabric with just the right amount of stretch, the Weapon Tee ensures you stay comfortable and stylish, whether you’re in the DJ booth, on the dancefloor, or repping your skills off-duty.

• 100% cotton, Airlume combed and ring-spun.

• Fabric weight: 4.3 oz./yd² (145.8 g/m²).

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for durability.

• Double-needle stitched sleeves and bottom hem for lasting quality.

• Tear-away label for comfort.
