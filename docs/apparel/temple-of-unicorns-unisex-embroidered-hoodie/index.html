Stay cozy while making a statement with the Temple of Unicorns Embroidered Hoodie. Featuring the iconic Temple of Unicorns logo embroidered on the chest, this classic unisex hoodie is perfect for music lovers, urban explorers, and fans of bold self-expression. Its soft cotton exterior and comfortable fit make it essential for cooler nights at festivals or lounging between sets.

Crafted with high-quality materials and designed to last, this hoodie offers the perfect balance of style and practicality.

• 65% ring-spun cotton, 35% polyester blend for durability and comfort.

• Charcoal Heather: 60% ring-spun cotton, 40% polyester.

• Carbon Grey: 55% ring-spun cotton, 45% polyester.

• 100% cotton face for a premium feel.

• Fabric weight: 8.5 oz./yd.² (288.2 g/m²).

• Front pouch pocket for convenience.

• Matching flat drawstrings and a 3-panel hood for a sleek look.

• Tear-away tag for added comfort.
