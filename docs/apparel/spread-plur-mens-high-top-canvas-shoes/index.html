Take every step with peace, love, unity, and respect in the Spread PLUR Men's High Top Canvas Shoes! Featuring a vibrant print of colorful kandi beads, these retro-style shoes bring rave culture to life in a bold and stylish way. Perfect for dance floors, festivals, or everyday streetwear, they’re a statement piece that lets your vibe shine from head to toe.

Whether you’re dancing under the lights or walking through the city, these shoes are made to spread good vibes everywhere you go.

• Faux leather toe cap

• Removable insole

• Padded collar, lace-up front
