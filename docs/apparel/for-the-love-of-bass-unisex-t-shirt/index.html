For The Love of Bass Unisex Tee

The For The Love of Bass Unisex Tee features a great big bass clef for fans of great big bass, perfect for music lovers who wear their passion on their sleeve—or chest! Whether you’re jamming to wubs at the club or keeping it casual in your headphones, this shirt blends comfort, style, and durability for any occasion.

Crafted from premium Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton, it’s lightweight, soft, and built to last. With pre-shrunk fabric and a flattering unisex fit, this tee is sure to become a wardrobe favorite.

• 100% cotton (except the heather grey color, which is a cotton/polyester blend)

• Airlume combed and ring-spun for sustainable comfort

• Pre-shrunk fabric for a consistent fit

• Built to last with side-seamed construction and shoulder-to-shoulder taping

• Made in America using an ecologically-responsible manufacturing process.

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²)
