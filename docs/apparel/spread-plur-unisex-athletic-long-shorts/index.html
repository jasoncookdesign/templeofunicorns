Spread PLUR Unisex Athletic Long Shorts

Move with peace, love, unity, and respect in the Spread PLUR Unisex Athletic Long Shorts! Featuring a vibrant kandi bead print, these versatile shorts are perfect for workouts, festivals, or laid-back days by the water. Their smooth, fast-drying, and breathable fabric ensures comfort whether you're running, swimming, or dancing.

Crafted from eco-friendly, recycled materials, these shorts combine style and sustainability. With mesh pockets for carrying valuables, four-way stretch for easy movement, and UPF50+ protection, they’re as functional as they are bold. Wherever your day takes you, these shorts help you spread PLUR with every step.

• 91% recycled polyester, 9% spandex

• Fabric weight: 5.13 oz. /yd. ² (174 g/m²)

• Four-way stretch moisture-wicking microfiber fabric

• Breathable and fast-drying material

• UPF50+ protection

• 6.5" (16.5 cm) inseam

• Elastic waistband with a flat white drawstring

• Mesh side pockets

• No inner lining

• Fabric is OEKO-TEX Standard 100 and Global Recycled Standard (GRS) certified
