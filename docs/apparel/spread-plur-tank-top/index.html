Spread PLUR Tank Top

Spread peace, love, unity, and respect with the vibrant energy of the Spread PLUR Tank Top! Featuring a kandi bead print and a message of positivity across the chest, this tank is perfect for outdoor festivals, sunny days, or layering your look.
