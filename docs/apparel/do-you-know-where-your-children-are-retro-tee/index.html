Ask about this design

Playfully challenge the suburban parenting anxieties of yesteryear with the 10 PM Retro TV T-Shirt! Featuring a vintage television displaying the iconic public service announcement "It's 10 PM. Do you know where your children are?", this design takes a nostalgic dig at the era of curfews and cautious parents. For ravers, the message carries an ironic twist—because let’s face it, the night doesn’t even begin until way past 10 PM.

Crafted from soft, lightweight cotton, this unisex tee blends retro aesthetics with a cheeky nod to the late-night culture of underground music scenes. Wear it for laughs, nostalgia, or as a badge of honor for living life after dark.

• Solid colors: 100% combed and ring-spun cotton (Heather colors include polyester).

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk for consistent fit and feel.

• Side-seamed construction for durability and style.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for added comfort.
