Ask about this design

Feel the bass and the comfort with the Wub Wub Wub Unisex Sweatshirt, perfect for keeping warm at festivals or lounging in between sets. It’s a versatile piece for bass lovers everywhere.

This sweatshirt is made from a cozy cotton-poly blend with air-jet spun yarn, offering unmatched softness, warmth, and durability. Its crease-free design and athletic rib-knit collar add to its polished yet laid-back vibe.

• 50% cotton, 50% polyester for a soft and warm feel.

• Fabric weight: 8.0 oz/yd² (271 g/m²) for a medium-heavy fit.

• Pre-shrunk for consistent sizing.

• 1x1 athletic rib-knit collar with spandex for added comfort and stretch.

• Air-jet spun yarn for reduced pilling and a smooth touch.

• Double-needle stitching for durability at the collar, shoulders, armholes, cuffs, and hem.

• Quarter-turned to avoid a crease down the middle.
