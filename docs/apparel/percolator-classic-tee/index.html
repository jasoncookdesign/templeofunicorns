Percolator Classic Tee

Step back in time while keeping the dance floor alive with the Percolator Classic Tee! Nostalgic with vintage imagery and a classic lyric, this Made-In-The-USA shirt is dripping with retro American charm.

This tee is perfect for fans of vintage aesthetics and second-wave house, making it a standout piece for casual wear or music events. Crafted from soft Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton, it offers timeless comfort and style to match its unforgettable design.

• Solid colors: 100% Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton (the grey heather color contains polyester).

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for reliable fit and feel.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for added structure.
