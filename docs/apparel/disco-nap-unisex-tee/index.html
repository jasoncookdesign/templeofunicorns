Disco Nap Unisex Tee

For those who nap hard and party harder, the Disco Nap Unisex Tee is your perfect match! The anime-style illustration of someone catching some Z’s on a nightclub couch is a cheeky warning about late-night energy management. We're meant to nap before going out – napping in the club just looks like passing out! Whether you’re repping rave culture or just embracing your love for cleverness and anime style, this tee is all about balance — because even the best nights out need a little rest.

Made from 100% Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton, the Disco Nap Unisex Tee offers premium comfort and durability. Lightweight, pre-shrunk, and polished with shoulder-to-shoulder taping, it’s perfect for festivals, casual hangs, or whenever you want your style to speak for itself.

• 100% cotton, Airlume combed and ring-spun (heather colors include polyester).

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for consistent sizing.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Tear-away label for added comfort.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for a polished finish.
