Taste the Rainbow Wide Leg Pants

By popular demand, the Taste the Rainbow design is now available as stylish wide-leg pants! At first glance, the pattern looks like classic polka dots, but look closer, and you'll uncover the playful hidden details that make these pants truly unique. Perfect for dancing, making a bold rave streetwear statement, or lounging in a cuddle puddle, these pants combine comfort and intrigue in one stylish package.

Crafted from premium knit mid-weight jersey fabric, these wide-leg pants offer a relaxed unisex fit with practical side pockets. The elastic waistband with a drawstring ensures a customizable and secure fit, whether you wear them on the waist or hips. Versatile, eye-catching, and oh-so-comfortable, they’re a must-have for anyone who loves functional fashion with a twist.

• Premium knit mid-weight jersey fabric for a smooth and stretchy feel.

• Fabric composition: 93% polyester, 7% spandex (US) / 96% polyester, 4% spandex (EU).

• Fabric weight: 6.34 oz./yd.² (EU) / 7.08 oz./yd.² (US).

• Relaxed unisex fit for ultimate comfort and style.

• Elastic waistband with a white drawstring for adjustability.

• Two side pockets for your essentials.

• OEKO-TEX 100 standard certified fabric for sustainability and quality.
