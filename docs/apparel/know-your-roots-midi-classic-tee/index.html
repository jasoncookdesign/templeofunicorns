Know Your Roots MIDI Classic Tee

Celebrate the origins of iconic electronic music with the Know Your Roots MIDI T-Shirt! Featuring a stylized MIDI piano roll of a legendary track, this tee pays homage to the beats that shaped a generation. Subtle yet striking, it’s perfect for producers, DJs, and fans who know ther classics.
