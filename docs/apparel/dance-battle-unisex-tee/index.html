Dance Battle Unisex Tee

Choose your vibe with the Dance Battle Unisex Tee! This bold design transforms the dancefloor into a fighting game character select screen, featuring nine stylized rave characters, each representing a different EDM subgenre. From tech titans to bass warriors, this tee is a celebration of the diversity and energy that makes rave culture unbeatable.

Made with soft, high-quality fabric, the Dance Battle Unisex Tee is perfect for festivals, gaming nights, or casual adventures. Declare your allegiance to the music, pick your fighter, and let your style be the ultimate champion.

• 100% Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for consistent sizing.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Tear-away label for added comfort.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for a polished finish.
