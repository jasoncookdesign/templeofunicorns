Ask about this design

Revisit the drama and the dance floors of 1989 with the Spaced Out! Second Summer of Love T-Shirt. Featuring an infamous headline and news article on underground raving, this tee captures the rebellious spirit of Britain's "Second Summer of Love." A perfect nod to the era when teenagers defied convention, danced till dawn, and made headlines for all the right (and wrong) reasons.

Made from ultra-soft cotton with a timeless unisex fit, this shirt lets you wear a piece of rave history while celebrating the freedom, fun, and underground vibes that defined the movement.

• 100% combed and ring-spun cotton (Heather colors include polyester) for a premium feel.

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for lasting fit and comfort.

• Side-seamed construction for durability and structure.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for added polish.
