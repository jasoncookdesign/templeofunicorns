Ask about this design

Welcome to Hard Beats University! Enroll in a world where the syllabus is all about spreading Peace, Love, Unity, and Respect, keeping the basslines hard and the vibes positive. This sweatshirt is your official uniform for majoring in the finer things in rave culture: shuffle dance, kandi bead jewelry-making, glowstick choreography, and festival logistics. Classes are always in session at Hard Beats University! Join the movement, wear the PLUR crest, and show the world you’re ready to make it a better, bass-filled place.

Crafted from a cozy cotton-polyester blend with a pre-shrunk design, this sweatshirt offers ultimate comfort and durability, whether you’re studying up on dance floor etiquette or teaching a masterclass on the perfect drop. With a crest proudly featuring PLUR symbols, you’ll be wearing the ultimate emblem of unity, self-expression, and good vibes.

• 50% cotton, 50% polyester for softness and warmth.

• Fabric weight: 8.0 oz./yd² (271 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for reliable fit and feel.

• 1x1 athletic rib-knit collar with spandex for added comfort and stretch.

• Air-jet spun yarn for reduced pilling and a smooth touch.

• Double-needle stitching for durability at the collar, shoulders, armholes, cuffs, and hem.

• Quarter-turned design to avoid creases down the middle.
