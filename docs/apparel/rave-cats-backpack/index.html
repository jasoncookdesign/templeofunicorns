Rave Cats Backpack

Carry your essentials with the playful energy of the Rave Cats Backpack! Featuring a adorable cats doing raver stuff, this medium-sized backpack is both stylish and functional. With plenty of room for your gear, including a separate compartment for a 15" laptop, this bag is designed to keep up with your adventures while adding a touch of feline fun.

Made from water-resistant polyester, the Rave Cats Backpack protects your belongings and ensures the vibrant print stays crisp, no matter the weather. Complete with ergonomic padded straps, a hidden back pocket for valuables, and silky lining, this backpack combines practicality with rave-ready charm.

• 100% polyester

• Water-resistant material

• Large inside pocket with a separate compartment for a 15" laptop, front pocket with a zipper, and a hidden pocket with zipper on the back of the bag

• Silky lining, piped inside hems, and a soft mesh back

• Padded ergonomic bag straps from polyester with plastic strap regulators
