A Bit More Underground Long Sleeve Stretch Crop Top

Celebrate your love for authentic music and bold fashion with the A Bit More Underground Long Sleeve Stretch Crop Top. Designed for those who value style as much as substance, this eco-friendly top champions the spirit of exploration — from underground venues to the streets of your city.

Made with recycled materials and featuring a sleek cropped fit, UPF 50+ sun protection, and a comfortable wide waistband, this top blends sustainability with a bold, unapologetic message. Perfect for warehouse dance floors, casual streetwear, or layering with your favorite looks, it’s a versatile piece for those who walk the path less traveled.

• 81% recycled polyester, 19% Lycra

• Fabric weight: 7.52 oz./yd.² (255 g/m²)

• UPF 50+

• Short cropped cut with a wide, double-layered waistline band and raglan sleeves

• Tear-away care label

• Snug stretch fit – size up if you’re between sizes

• Fabric is OEKO-TEX 100 Standard and Global Recycled Standard (GRS) certified
