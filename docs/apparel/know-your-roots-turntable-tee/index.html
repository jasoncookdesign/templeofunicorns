Ask about this design

Celebrate the workhorse of DJ culture and electronic music with the Know Your Roots Turntable T-Shirt! Featuring the turntable that EVERYONE used from the '70s through the '00s, this tee pays homage to the hardware that revolutionized sound and shaped generations of beats. Striking and not at all understated, this tee is perfect for vinyl lovers, DJs, and fans who know their classics.

Crafted from premium combed and ring-spun cotton, this shirt feels soft, lightweight, and just stretchy enough for all-day comfort. Its timeless design and flattering fit make it a must-have for music lovers who understand the roots of the sound they love.

• 100% combed and ring-spun cotton (Heather colors include polyester).

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for consistent sizing.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for a polished finish.
