Step into the spotlight with the Neon Anime Club Tank! Featuring bold, neon-colored dancers in a vibrant, painterly '90s anime style, this all-over print tank captures the electrifying energy of a packed nightclub. Whether you're on the dancefloor, at a festival, or just turning heads on a sunny day, this tank brings the party with you.

Crafted from smooth, premium polyester and spandex fabric, the Neon Nightclub Tank offers a relaxed fit with 2-way stretch for maximum comfort and durability. Perfect for layering or standing out on its own, it’s a bold statement for lovers of dance music and striking design.

• 93% polyester, 7% spandex

Fabric weight: 7.08 oz./yd.² (240 g/m²)

• Premium knit mid-weight jersey

• Smooth, comfortable 2-way stretch material stretches and recovers on the cross and lengthwise grains

• OEKO-TEX 100 standard certified fabric
