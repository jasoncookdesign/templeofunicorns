DJ Pinup Unisex Tee

Turn up the retro charm with the DJ Pinup Unisex Tee! Combining vintage vibes with dance music culture, this shirt is a stylish nod to timeless beats and bold self-expression. Perfect for music lovers who appreciate a touch of nostalgia in their wardrobe.

Made from 100% Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton, the DJ Pinup Unisex Tee is lightweight, durable, and designed for comfort. Whether you’re at a festival, in the club, or keeping it casual, this tee lets your love for music and style shine.

• 100% cotton, Airlume combed and ring-spun.

• Fabric weight: 4.2 oz./yd.² (142 g/m²).

• Pre-shrunk fabric for consistent sizing.

• Side-seamed construction for durability.

• Tear-away label for added comfort.

• Shoulder-to-shoulder taping for a polished finish.
